Clear Answers on Winter Smog & Safety

Why is smog significantly worse between 6:00 AM and 10:00 AM? expand_more

Winter nocturnal radiation cools the ground, creating a thermal inversion ceiling. Cooler air gets trapped below warm air, preventing particulate from dispersing upward until solar radiation warms the surface layer near midday.

Does Gemini provide certified medical diagnoses? expand_more

No. AeroPulse delivers environmental risk synthesis strictly based on atmospheric data, official EPA health standards, and published indoor ventilation science. It never substitutes licensed clinical guidance.

Can regular cloth masks filter toxic PM2.5? expand_more

Cloth bandanas and surgical masks filter under 20% of sub-micron particles. For dangerous AQI levels exceeding 150+, only tight-fitting certified respirators (N95, FFP2, or KF94) provide effective protection.
