MODULE 02 Why Is Winter Smog So Dangerous? Meteorological Physics

The deadly convergence of boundary layer thermal inversion, moisture condensation, and stagnant geographic bowls during cold seasons.

Boundary Layer Dynamics: Normal Dispersion vs. Inversion Cap Thermal Physics
Normal / Warm Season airwave

Ground heats up; buoyant warm air parcels naturally ascend into colder upper layers, clearing ground-level smoke and particulates into higher troposphere currents.

Winter Thermal Inversion compress

Ground radiates heat overnight rapidly. Cold heavy air sinks into basins beneath a stationary ceiling of warmer air. The warm layer acts as a physical hermetic lid trapping 100% of emissions.

info Boundary Layer Compression: During extreme winter smog events, the mixing boundary height collapses from 2,000 meters down to less than 150 meters, concentrating emissions by up to 1,300%.
The 4 Winter Multipliers

Why seasonal air turns from moderately poor to hazardous overnight.

air
Stagnant Wind Velocity (< 5 km/h) Zero Horizontal Wash

Absence of surface pressure gradients prevents advective ventilation, causing local combustion to compound linearly hour by hour.

water_drop

High nocturnal humidity enables aqueous photochemical reactions, converting gaseous SOx and NOx into heavy hygroscopic secondary sulfates.

fireplace

Coal and unvented wood fires release particulates directly at breathing level (0 to 3 meters), bypassing taller industrial exhaust dispersion.

MODULE 03 International AQI Matrix: The Breakpoint Disparity

Why an AQI of "100" in Delhi is vastly different from "100" in London or Los Angeles. Decoupling mathematical normalizations from physical mass density.

01. AVERAGING WINDOWS

US-EPA NowCast utilizes a weighted rolling 12-hour average. India NAQI applies a strict 24-hour arithmetic mean. Real-time instantaneous spikes are smoothed differently.

02. BREAKPOINT ALGORITHMS

In the US, 35.4 µg/m³ of PM2.5 triggers AQI 101 ("Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups"). In India NAQI, AQI 100 corresponds to 60 µg/m³, almost double the particle mass.

03. WHO STRICT BENCHMARKS

WHO does NOT publish an index (0–500). Instead, it defines biological absolute thresholds: 15 µg/m³ (24h) and 5 µg/m³ (Annual mean).

Atmospheric pollutants do not remain confined to respiration. Ultrafine particles catalyze systemic inflammatory cascades throughout vital organs.

Risk Multipliers

Elevated arterial vasoconstriction induces acute myocardial infarction within 1 to 2 hours of extreme exposure spikes.
